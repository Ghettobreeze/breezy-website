From the founder

Hi, I'm Shawn Breeze.

I started Breezy Career Services because I've watched too many good, hardworking people get passed over. They aren't short on talent. They just never had anyone in their corner showing them how to tell their story, where the better jobs are, or how to ask for what they're worth.

I believe success shouldn't depend on who you know. A strong resume, a real plan and someone who believes in you can change a career, and a better career can change a family. Raising your income, starting a business, owning what you build: that's how generational wealth begins. One step, one paycheck, one decision at a time.

That's why I work with people at every stage. A graduate writing a first resume. A professional who's underpaid and ready to move up. A business owner who's great at the work but whose customers can't find them. Everyone deserves a shot, and price shouldn't be the thing that stops anyone. That's why every request for hardship help gets my personal attention.

When you work with Breezy, you work with me. I'll be honest about where you stand, I'll do the work with you, and everything we build together is yours to keep. When you win, your family wins and your community wins. That's the whole point.

Shawn Breeze, Founder
